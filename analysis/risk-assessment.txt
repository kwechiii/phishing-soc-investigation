CASE ID: PHISH-001
ASSESSMENT: Phishing Incident Risk Assessment
EVIDENCE TYPE: Simulated training scenario

THREAT:
Credential phishing leading to unauthorized Microsoft 365 access.

VULNERABILITIES / CONTROL GAPS:
1. Employee entered credentials into a phishing website.
2. Unfamiliar sign-in succeeded without an MFA requirement.
3. DLP policy operated in AuditOnly mode.
4. Sensitive financial information was downloaded.

CURRENT RISK ASSESSMENT:
Likelihood: 3/3 (High)
Impact: 3/3 (High)
Risk Score: 9/9 (High)

RISK RATIONALE:
The simulated incident includes confirmed unauthorized
account access and a download of sensitive financial data.
Further distribution of the data is not established.

NIST SP 800-53 CONTROL MAPPING:

IA-2 - Identification and Authentication
Review MFA requirements and authentication exceptions.

AC-2 - Account Management
Disable or secure compromised accounts and revoke sessions.

SI-4 - System Monitoring
Monitor suspicious sign-ins and unusual account activity.

AU-6 - Audit Record Review, Analysis, and Reporting
Correlate authentication, email, and file-access events.

AC-4 - Information Flow Enforcement
Evaluate restrictions on sensitive-data transfers.

RECOMMENDED REMEDIATION:
1. Reset affected credentials and revoke active sessions.
2. Investigate unfamiliar sign-ins and connected devices.
3. Strengthen phishing-resistant MFA where feasible.
4. Review DLP policy enforcement and appropriate exceptions.
5. Alert on suspicious access to sensitive financial files.
6. Provide targeted phishing-awareness training.

PROPOSED RESIDUAL RISK:
Likelihood: 1/3 (Low)
Impact: 3/3 (High)
Risk Score: 3/9 (Medium)

NOTE:
Residual risk is a projected assessment, not a measured
post-remediation outcome. Controls have not been implemented
or validated in this simulated scenario.
